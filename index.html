How It Works

One scan. Complete trust.

Every animal on Oriva carries a QR passport that stays live for its whole life on the farm - and follows it to its next home.

  1. 01

    Tag & register

    Print the QR tag, register the animal with its unique Tag ID, and its digital passport is live in minutes.

  2. 02

    Record the daily rhythm

    Milk, health, feed, expenses - quick forms take seconds, and every entry lands on the right animal's record automatically.

  3. 03

    Sell with proof

    List on the marketplace or deal in person - buyers scan the QR and see the verified history straight from your farm.
