01
Herd Registry

A living passport for every animal.

Every animal on your farm gets a unique Tag ID and a dynamic QR code that stays current for its entire life. Breed, age, weight, lineage, lactation stage and a photo history - all in one profile that updates with every milking, vaccination and weight check.

  • Unique Tag ID per animalPrint QR tags as ear tags or neck collars - scan to open the full live profile.
  • Lineage trackingDam, sire, siblings and offspring - every relationship mapped automatically.
  • Photo timelineSnap pictures at every stage - buyers see the animal grow over its lifetime.

02
Health Records

Every jab and treatment, on record.

Vaccinations, deworming, antibiotics, vet visits - nothing gets forgotten, nothing gets disputed. Schedule FMD, HS and deworming cycles so the system reminds you weeks ahead. Every treatment is timestamped, vet-signed and QR-verifiable by any buyer.

  • Vaccination calendarFMD, HS, brucellosis, deworming - automated reminders two weeks before each dose.
  • Treatment logsDrug name, dosage, vet name, withdrawal period - all recorded in 30 seconds.
  • Buyer-verifiable recordsScan the tag and the buyer sees the full history - no questions, no haggling.
03
Production Tracking

Morning & evening, per animal.

Log AM and PM yields per cow or for the whole herd in seconds. Daily totals, weekly trends, monthly comparisons and best-producer rankings - all automatic. Spot a drop the day it happens, not when you see it in the month-end numbers.

  • AM / PM loggingTwo-tap entry per cow - the system handles totals, averages and rankings.
  • Weekly & monthly trendsCompare against last week, last month, or the same week last year.
  • Top-producer rankingsKnow which cows carry your farm - and which need attention.
