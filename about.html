Where It All Started

A notebook, a missed opportunity, and a better idea.

In the winter of 2023, our founding team was visiting a dairy farm in Sahiwal when we witnessed something that changed everything. A buyer had travelled three hours to purchase a prized Sahiwal cow - and walked away empty-handed because the farmer couldn't produce a verifiable vaccination record. The notebook where the record lived was at the farmhouse, five kilometres away.

That moment crystallised a truth we'd been circling around: Pakistan's dairy industry, worth billions of rupees, was still running on paper registers, WhatsApp messages and memory. Farmers were losing money not because their animals weren't good, but because they couldn't prove it.

We didn't want to build another app. We wanted to build a passport for every animal - and a marketplace where that passport meant something.

The Gap We Fill

The two sides of the same coin.

Every broken system creates an opportunity to build something fairer. Here's the problem we found - and the solution we're delivering.

The Problem we saw

Every dairy farm we visited had the same pain points, repeated across Punjab and beyond:

  • Paper registers scattered across notebooks - milk in one, medicine in another, money in someone's head
  • No way to prove an animal's history to buyers - no vaccine record, no lineage proof, no milk yield data
  • Brokers taking 8-15% commission on every sale because farmers had no direct access to buyers
  • Dips in milk production noticed weeks too late - costing thousands of rupees

The Solution we built

Oriva Dairy combines six tools into one simple system that any farmer can use, regardless of size or tech literacy:

  • One digital passport per animal with a dynamic QR code that lives for its entire lifetime
  • Vaccination and health records buyers can verify in one scan - building instant trust
  • Built-in marketplace with zero commissions - farmers deal directly with buyers
  • Daily milk analytics that flag dips the same day they happen - not a month later

Our Mission

To give every dairy farmer in Pakistan - from the 5-cow family farm to the 500-head operation - the digital tools and market access they need to run a profitable, transparent, and traceable business. We believe a farmer's reputation should be provable, and their hard work should be paid fairly.

Our Vision

A dairy industry where every litre of milk, every animal sold, and every rupee earned can be traced back to its source - bringing trust to buyers, transparency to farmers, and safety to consumers. We see Oriva as the backbone of Pakistan's dairy supply chain within a decade.

How We Work

Six values we won't compromise.

Every feature we build, every farmer we onboard, and every partnership we form is measured against these principles.

Farmer-first

Every decision starts with a simple question: does this make the farmer's life easier and their business more profitable? If not, we don't build it.

Trust by design

We verify records, we don't alter them. We protect data like it's our own. A farmer's information stays theirs - always - and is only shared when they choose.

Radically simple

Our users are farmers, not techies. If a feature needs a manual, it's not finished. We obsess over making powerful tools feel effortless.

Fair markets

Zero commissions isn't a marketing line - it's a promise. We believe the money a farmer earns belongs to the farmer, not to middlemen.

Long-term thinking

We're building for decades, not quarters. Our cattle passports are designed to last a lifetime. Our partnerships are built on mutual growth.

Community-led

We learn from the farmers we serve. Every feature request, every complaint, every success story shapes what Oriva becomes next.
