How It Works

Three steps. A lifetime of proof.

From the day an animal is registered to the day it's sold, its passport builds a verifiable story that no one can dispute.

01

Tag & Register

Print the QR tag, attach it as an ear tag or neck collar, and register the animal with its unique Tag ID. Its digital passport goes live in minutes.

What you enter: Breed, date of birth, dam/sire IDs, initial weight, photo. Time: ~5 minutes per animal.
02

Record the Daily Rhythm

Milk, health, feed, expenses - quick forms take seconds, and every entry lands on the right animal's record automatically. The passport grows richer every day.

Auto-tracked: Milk yield (AM/PM), vaccinations, treatments, weight changes, lactation stage.
03

Sell with Proof

List on the marketplace or deal in person - buyers scan the QR and see the verified history straight from your farm. No haggling over "what do you have?"

Buyer sees: Full health record, milk history, lineage, photo timeline - all timestamped and farm-verified.

Inside the Passport

Everything a buyer needs, in one scan.

When a buyer scans the QR code, they don't just see a name and breed. They see the animal's entire life - verified, timestamped, and impossible to fake.

  • Unique Identity Tag ID, breed, date of birth, colour markings, and photo history that updates as the animal grows.
  • Complete Health Record Every vaccination, deworming, treatment, and vet visit - with dates, dosages, and vet signatures.
  • Production History Daily milk yields, lactation cycles, peak production periods, and current yield vs. lifetime average.
  • Lineage & Offspring Dam, sire, siblings, and calves - proving genetic quality and breeding value.

Security & Privacy

Your data is yours. Always.

Traceability doesn't mean exposure. We built Oriva so farmers control exactly what buyers see - and what stays private.

Farmer-Controlled Access

You decide what's visible. Health records, milk yields, and expenses can be set to "private" or "buyer-visible" per animal. You're always in control.

Tamper-Proof Records

Every entry is timestamped and linked to your farm ID. Records can't be edited after verification - buyers know what they see is real.

Your Data, Your Export

Export your complete farm data anytime. Delete your account and everything goes with you. We never sell, rent, or share your information.
